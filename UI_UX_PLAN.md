# Bible Memorization Companion - Design Brief

## 1. Why This Document Exists
This brief is for the designer creating the app mockups.
It explains the product in simple terms, what people should feel when using it, and what to design first.

## 2. Product Overview
Bible Memorization Companion is a mobile app for iPhone and Android.

The goal is simple: help people memorize Bible verses through short, repeatable study sessions.

The app is free to download.

In the first release:
- Scripture content is free.
- People can start studying without creating an account.
- The experience should feel clear, calm, and easy.

Later releases add paid options:
- Audio content
- Quiz content

## 3. Who This Is For
Primary audience:
- Bible Quiz students (teens and adults)

Secondary audience:
- Parents, coaches, and church leaders supporting students

## 4. Language and Markets
- Launch focus: English and Spanish users globally
- The app should automatically follow the device language
- If the device language is not supported, default to English
- People can still download Scripture content in either supported language

## 5. Core Experience We Want
The main user journey should feel fast and natural:
1. Pick Scripture content
2. Study verse by verse
3. Come back daily and keep making progress

The app should remove friction:
- No forced sign-up when first opening the app
- Easy entry into study mode
- Clear status for every content item (ready, downloaded, locked, etc.)

## 6. Key Business Rules
- App download is free
- Scripture content is free
- People can browse and download free Scripture without an account
- Account is only required when needed for paid features or purchase recovery
- If someone tries an action that needs an account, ask them to sign in at that moment
- After sign-in, send them back to what they were trying to do

## 7. Content Organization
Scripture should be offered in two ways:
1. Full Bible book
2. Bible Quiz season set

Every content card should show:
- Title
- Language
- Book/season label
- Download status
- File size
- Last update date

## 8. Main App Areas to Design
1. My Studies
2. Store
3. Library
4. Study
5. Progress
6. Settings

My Studies is the main starting point and should guide people to their next action.

## 9. Study Mode (First Release)
Verse-by-verse mode is the heart of the app.

Expected behavior:
- Show verse reference first
- Tap to reveal verse text
- Tap again to hide text
- Swipe to move to next or previous verse
- Show where the learner is in the current section

**Status: done.** This brief has been executed — the mockups exist

The first design package should include clickable mockups for:
1. Welcome/My Studies entry (guest-friendly)
2. My Studies with Continue Studying as the main action
3. Store list (free Scripture content)
4. Content detail screen
5. Content status states (free, owned, locked, downloaded)
6. Download states (downloading, success, failed, retry)
7. Library (downloaded content)
8. Verse study screen (tap and swipe behavior)
9. Sign-in prompt shown only when needed
10. Settings (text size and theme)
11. Progress screen (on this device)
12. Audio teaser area (preview only, no full audio flow yet)

Not part of first mockups:
- Full quiz experience
- Full audio purchase and playback experience
- Cross-device progress sync

## 11. Second Mockup Package (After MVP)
Include:
1. Audio purchase flow
2. Audio playback experience
3. Quiz setup and quiz session flow
4. Question-type purchase options
5. Purchase recovery flow
6. Purchase success and failure states

## 12. Third Mockup Package (Later Growth)
Include:
1. Progressive recall mode (harder recall practice)
2. Deeper learning insights
3. Motivation features (streaks, milestones)
4. Optional account-based sync screens

## 13. Brand and Tone Direction
Visual direction:
- Minimal
- Calm
- Respectful
- Focused on Scripture readability

Writing tone:
- Plain
- Friendly
- Encouraging
- Not overly formal

## 14. Accessibility and Inclusivity
The design should support a wide range of users:
- Clear contrast
- Comfortable text reading
- Text size controls
- Easy-to-tap buttons
- Works well with screen readers

## 15. Success Checklist for Design Review
The first mockups are successful if:
1. A new user can start studying quickly without sign-up friction
2. Content status is obvious at a glance
3. Verse study actions are self-explanatory
4. Users can study downloaded content without internet

## 16. Decisions Already Final
These points are already agreed:
1. First release is guest-first
2. App language follows device language, with English fallback
3. Users can use mixed content languages
4. Scripture is grouped by full book and BQ season set
5. Paid items are sold as one-time purchases per package
6. Quiz products are sold by question type
7. Purchase recovery starts from the Store header
8. My Studies is the main hub for navigation
9. Target date for first mockups: July 20, 2026
